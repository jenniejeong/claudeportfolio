About Me

Hi, I’m Jennie!

I’m a student at UC Berkeley studying Cognitive Science, Data Science, and Design Innovation, and I love spending time thinking about how people think and why we’re drawn to certain design choices from a psychological and neurological perspective.

I work across product design, management, and UX, discovering what users actually need, the stuff they don’t always say out loud, and shaping it into products that feel clear, human, and genuinely useful. I have experience working on diverse tech for social good projects across education and am always looking for more ways to connect tech with small businesses to make more intentional design choices.
