About Me

Hi, I’m Jennie!

I’m an undergraduate student at UC Berkeley studying Cognitive Science, Data Science, and Design. I work across product design, strategy, and UX research with a belief that great products are built on radical clarity, user empathy, and real-world impact.

My work centers around uncovering what users truly need and translating those insights into clean, intentional product experiences. Whether I’m conducting user research, shaping product strategy, or designing end-to-end interactions, I always prioritize purpose over guesswork.
